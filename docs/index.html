How it works

This tracks a Nuzlocke run. It doesn't roll quests — hunt whatever you like, then come back and say what happened.

  1. Get a combo. Either the app hands you a surviving weapon and style, or you pick one yourself. Dead combos are never offered.
  2. Name the quest you're about to hunt.
  3. Report the result — Cleared, Carted, or Failed. Carted can be pressed more than once; it doesn't end the attempt.

The kill condition you chose is checked against that result. When it fires, the weapon/style combo you were using is struck off the board permanently.

It's one choice rather than several. Carting and failing a quest are separate things — you can time out or blow a sub-objective without ever carting, and you can cart twice and still clear — so the harshest setting covers both explicitly. Below that, each rule already covers the gentler ones beneath it.

Arena quests don't count. Their weapons are handed to you, so nothing is lost on an Arena hunt.

Locks are on by default and are what the difficulty scale is measured against. The weapon/style lock means you can't swap at all until that combo falls. The quest lock means a quest that beat you is owed one retry — your next hunt has to be that quest, win or lose. Turning either off makes the run easier and scores lower.

The run ends when nothing is left standing.

Other MHGU Apps

Companion fan tools by the same author. Each opens in a new tab.

  • Quest Randomizer Rolls random quests when you can't decide what to hunt.
  • Collection Tracker Tracks which weapons, armor and Palico gear you own, and what it costs to finish them.

  • Bingo Builds a bingo card of hunting goals, with a shareable seed so a group can race the same board.
  • Hunting Log Keeps a running record of your hunts — what you were after, what you wore, who came along.
